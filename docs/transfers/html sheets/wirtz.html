Florian Wirtz Likely Heading to Liverpool

Florian Wirtz, the 22-year-old German attacking midfielder from Bayer Leverkusen, is reportedly on the verge of joining Liverpool FC. The Reds have agreed on personal terms with Wirtz, positioning themselves as front-runners in the race for his signature.

Liverpool has officially begun formal negotiations with Bayer Leverkusen to sign Wirtz. The potential transfer fee is expected to be in the range of €130–150 million (£110–125 million), which would make Wirtz the most expensive German player in history.

Wirtz's decision to favor Liverpool over Bayern Munich has reportedly left the German champions stunned, as they believed they had a commitment from the player for a future move.

The transfer is expected to be finalized soon, with Liverpool aiming to wrap up the deal quickly.

Wirtz's addition to Liverpool's midfield, alongside Dominik Szoboszlai, is anticipated to form one of the most exciting and inventive midfield duos in European football.

As the summer transfer window approaches, Liverpool fans eagerly await official confirmation of Wirtz's signing, which could mark a significant step in the club's ongoing efforts to maintain their status at the pinnacle of European football.
